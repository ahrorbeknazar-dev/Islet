Interventions Everything here changes the trace within seconds

Insulin

4.0 U
Rapid: onset ~15 min, peak ~65 min, gone by ~4–5 h. Dose twice too soon and it stacks.

Food

Fast carbs are the “rule of 15” rescue: juice or glucose tabs, absorbed in minutes.

Activity

Working muscle pulls in glucose without needing insulin — GLUT4 moves to the membrane on contraction alone.

Emergency

Glucagon only works if the liver still has glycogen to release. Watch the glycogen meter before you use it.

Automated insulin delivery

A pump reading the sensor every 5 minutes, adjusting itself. This is what changed daily life for a lot of people.

Type 2 medicines

Each acts on a different part of the loop. Hover the trace to see which one bends it.
This is a teaching model, not medical advice. The physiology is simplified, the constants are tuned for legibility on screen, and the doses are illustrative. Real insulin dosing depends on an individual's sensitivity, ratios, and clinical guidance. Nothing here should ever be used to make a treatment decision. If you or someone you love has diabetes, work with your care team.
